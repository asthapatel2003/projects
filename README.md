## 🚀 **Project Overview**

Healthcare systems often face the challenge of **resource inefficiency** — underutilized staff, unbalanced patient flow, and uneven revenue streams.  
Our project, **Smart Staff**, introduces a **data-driven decision system** to optimize staffing strategies and vendor choices.
